  square fails the WHOLE scene compile; bisect at the REAL layer
  (create_session — compile_authorable_runtime skips the concourse).
- QUEUE-PORTAL: resolves from point/width ALONE; ON a wall face the
  one-sided tiles leave portals_named empty; rails stay DRESS.
- ADMISSION-CAP: cap=1 admits exactly ONE agent ever — pin cap =
  the slot count. CHASE-HOST: the tail spawns in zones[0]'s strips —
  order the zones so the queue's host is zone0 + re-author its box.
- CONGESTION DRAG: realized speed decays as aisles pack — start the
  plan high. RELEASE-CYCLE: a line ALONG a busy corridor HOLDS (0
  releases) — the parked line IS the beat; amend the band with cause.
- EYE-LADDER: practicals ride _lw=3.5 under Cycles incl. lanterns;
  aerial needs exposure ~0.85 + canopies smaller than the light
  spread; _kf PRESERVES z (cranes need _kf_loc3); EVERY moving lens
  gets keys; CAST carriers must NOT be hide_render; the stills runner
  needs the FULL env.
- GOVERNED-COPY: governed-copy syncs are COMMITS; never rsync
  --delete into a superset dir (prop_carry class).
- MARKET-SHAPED GATES: non-commute films re-author their probe gates
  from the measurement (arrivals / aisle spread / linger / queue).
